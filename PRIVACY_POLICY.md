# Privacy Policy for MehndiGo

**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

At **MehndiGo** (referred to as "we", "us", "our", or "Company"), we value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, share, and protect your personal information when you use the **MehndiGo** mobile application (available on Android and iOS platforms) and our website [https://mehndigo.in](https://mehndigo.in) (collectively, the "Platform").

MehndiGo is an online platform that connects customers looking for professional Mehndi services with verified Mehndi artists ("Artists"). 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, registering with, accessing, or using the Platform, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not download, register, or use the Platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary for the performance of our services, optimization of the Platform, and compliance with our legal obligations. The categories of information we collect include:

### A. Information You Provide to Us

*   **Account Registration Details:** Full name, mobile number, email address, gender, profile photo (optional), and login credentials.
*   **Location and Address Information:** Complete billing and service delivery address, including flat/house number, street name, city, state, and PIN code.
*   **Artist Verification & Business Details:** Government-issued identification documents (for artists, e.g., Aadhaar, PAN card, or GST details, where applicable), business/portfolio details, service listings, pricing details, and artist experience.
*   **Media and Uploads:** Photos of past work or portfolios (for artists) and reference design images uploaded by users during booking or chats.
*   **Feedback & Communications:** Customer reviews, ratings, suggestions, support ticket descriptions, and communications via in-app chat or email.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

*   **Precise GPS Location Information:** With your explicit permission, we collect precise GPS coordinates to show nearby artists, verify service locations, and optimize bookings.
*   **Device Information:** Mobile device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID), mobile network operator, and system performance metrics.
*   **Usage & Analytics Data:** Log files, page response times, app usage patterns, pages visited, session duration, and features used.
*   **Technical Identifiers:** IP address, internet browser type/app version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens.
*   **Diagnostics & Crash Reports:** Automated diagnostic reports, crash logs, and performance statistics to debug app issues.

### C. Information from Third Parties

*   **Payment Details:** Payments are securely processed through trusted payment processors (e.g., Razorpay). We receive transactional statuses (e.g., payment success/failure), but we do **not** receive or store sensitive data like credit card numbers, CVVs, cardholder names, or bank passwords.

## 2. Device Permissions Used

To provide a fully functional and secure experience, the Platform requests specific device permissions. You can enable or disable these permissions at any time through your device settings:

| Permission | Purpose | Impact of Denial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location (GPS)** | To identify nearby Mehndi artists, pre-fill service addresses, and calculate travel fees. | You will need to manually enter your location/address, and some distance-based services may be unavailable. |
| **Camera** | To allow artists to take portfolio photos and customers to upload reference designs or profile pictures directly. | You will not be able to capture and upload photos in real-time. |
| **Gallery / Photos** | To select existing portfolio designs, profile pictures, or booking references from your device's photo library. | You will not be able to choose and upload saved images. |
| **Notifications (FCM)** | To send push notifications for booking confirmations, real-time artist updates, secure payment alerts, and chat messages. | You will miss important real-time updates and will need to check the app manually. |
| **Internet / Network** | To connect the application with our servers and load map resources, chat services, and payment screens. | The app will not work, as it requires an active internet connection. |
| **Storage (Read/Write)** | To cache temporary app files, save downloaded receipts, and load high-resolution portfolio images quickly. | Performance may be slower, and files/receipts cannot be saved directly to the device. |

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for the following purposes based on our contractual obligations to you, our legitimate business interests, and legal requirements:

1.  **Providing & Managing Services:** To create and maintain accounts, verify artists, manage bookings, coordinate service schedules, and facilitate contact between artists and customers.
2.  **Payment Facilitation:** To securely process booking amounts, platform fees, and artist payouts through our integrated payment gateways.
3.  **Real-Time Alerts:** To send transaction confirmations, booking reminders, security alerts, and customer service updates via push notifications, SMS, or email.
4.  **Service Personalization:** To recommend nearby Mehndi artists and match customer requests with artist availability.
5.  **Platform Improvement:** To analyze usage trends, identify system bottlenecks, monitor performance, and develop new features using anonymized analytics.
6.  **Customer Support:** To resolve booking disputes, debug technical errors, and respond to inquiries.
7.  **Fraud Prevention & Security:** To detect and prevent suspicious logins, identity theft, financial fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service.
8.  **Marketing & Promotional Communications:** Only with your opt-in consent, we may send promotional offers, newsletters, or seasonal discounts. You can opt out at any time.

## 4. Payment Information Security

All financial transactions on MehndiGo are handled through secure, third-party payment gateway providers (such as **Razorpay**). 
*   MehndiGo does **not** collect, store, or view your sensitive payment card details, bank account passwords, or CVV codes.
*   All payment processing complies with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).
*   Data transmission between our app and the payment gateway is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) and Transport Layer Security (TLS) technology.

---

## 5. Location Information Usage

We collect location data only when you authorize the app to access location services.
*   **Geographical Matching:** We use GPS coordinates to show customers a list of Mehndi artists active in their immediate vicinity and to calculate distance-based pricing.
*   **Artist Tracking:** For artists, location details verify that the artist has arrived at the booking location for safety and service validation.
*   **Background Location:** MehndiGo does **not** track your location in the background when the app is closed unless explicitly enabled for service verification (e.g., an artist actively traveling to a booking).

## 6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do **not sell or rent your personal information to third parties**. We only share your data under the following circumstances:

1.  **With Mehndi Artists / Customers:** Once a booking is confirmed, we share relevant contact information (Name, Phone Number, Service Address) with the booked artist or customer to facilitate service completion.
2.  **With Third-Party Service Providers:** We share necessary data with trusted vendors who perform operational services on our behalf:
    *   **Payment Gateways:** Razorpay (to process payment transactions).
    *   **Cloud Infrastructure:** Hosting and storage providers (e.g., Firebase, AWS, or Google Cloud).
    *   **Communication Services:** FCM (for push notifications) and transactional SMS/email delivery services.
    *   **Maps & Geolocation:** Google Maps API (to convert GPS coordinates to addresses).
    *   **Analytics & Debugging:** Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Google Analytics.
3.  **For Legal Compliance:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority (such as tax compliance, law enforcement queries, or regulatory audits).
4.  **Business Transfers:** If the Company undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale, user databases may be transferred as part of the business assets, subject to the same privacy commitments.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, organizational, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data:
*   **HTTPS Encryption:** All network traffic between the mobile application and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.
*   **Access Control:** Access to personal user databases is strictly restricted to authorized employees who require it for operational purposes.
*   **Token Authentication:** We use secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and multi-factor authentication (where applicable) to verify user identity.
*   **Secure Cloud Hosting:** All database entries and uploaded media are stored on secure cloud servers featuring active firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular backups.
*   **Constant Auditing:** We regularly review our data collection, storage, and processing practices to identify and fix potential system vulnerabilities.

However, please be aware that no transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention & Deletion

*   **Retention Period:** We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. 
*   **Legal Compliance:** We may retain certain transaction data or identity verification records for longer periods if required to satisfy legal, accounting, tax, or regulatory requirements.
*   **Account Deletion:** You can request account deletion at any time through the in-app settings screen or by emailing our support team. Upon receiving your deletion request:
    *   We will delete or permanently anonymize your personal identification data.
    *   Some anonymized usage history and transaction summaries may persist in backups or analytical logs.
    *   Your profile will be removed from public view immediately.
